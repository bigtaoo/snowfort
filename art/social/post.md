## Reddit / LinkedIn / Discord (longer)

**Snow Fort Showdown — a couch snowball war you can play in the browser**

I made a small same-device 2-player game for Playgama's "Player Two Found" challenge:

- You both get two minutes to build a fort from snow, ice and wood blocks, and hide your snowman king inside.
- Then take turns firing a catapult: lock the aim, hold to charge, release to throw.
- Real physics: walls topple, ice cracks, and a lucky shot can bring the whole fort down on the king.
- First to three round wins takes the match, with repairs in between. No friend nearby? Play against Frosty, the CPU.

Works with keyboard/mouse and touch (landscape), and in 15 languages.

Play here: https://playgama.ai/play/dqq7iq6dcikez6

The challenge winner is decided by likes, so if you enjoy it, a ❤️ on the game page helps a lot. Feedback is very welcome!
